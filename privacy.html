Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

OpenList Personal is used only by the owner of this site, with their own Google account.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app accesses files in the owner's Google Drive so they can be listed, opened and saved through OpenList on the owner's own computer.

Where data goes

Files and login tokens stay between Google and the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to, stored on or shared with any other server or person. No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
